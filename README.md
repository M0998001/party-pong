# Party Pong

A colorful pong game made for a tablet in the middle of the table, for 2–4 people at once.

**Play it:** https://m0998001.github.io/party-pong/

- **2 players** on a rectangle, **3** on a triangle, **4** on a square. Everyone drags along their own edge.
- **Target Practice:** a solo 60-second mode with combos and a saved best score.
- CPU players with Easy / Medium / Hard skill.
- 8 power-ups, 8 optional modifiers and 6 obstacle maps.
- Earn coins as you play and spend them in the shop on paddles, balls, trails, arenas and maps.

It's a single self-contained `index.html`. Coins and unlocks are saved in the browser on each device.
Works with a keyboard too: W/S, ↑/↓, ←/→ and J/L move players 1–4; P or Esc pauses.
